Who We Are

The Bridge Between Solar & Roofing

Are you a roofing contractor tired of dealing with solar panels slowing down your jobs? Or a homeowner who needs their roof replaced but isn't sure what happens to their solar system?

At Solar Detach & Reset, we specialize exclusively in removing and reinstalling solar panel systems for roof repair or replacement. We don't sell solar. We don't sell roofs. We simply ensure your solar panels are safely handled so your roofing project goes smoothly.

  • No broken tiles — meticulous panel handling
  • Properly sealed mounts every time
  • Remarkable communication throughout
  • Veteran & first responder discounts
