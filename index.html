About Our Service

Solar Panel Removal & Reinstallation Across Arizona

When your Phoenix home needs a new roof, the solar panels on top of it don't have to become a headache. Solar Detach & Reset is Arizona's dedicated solar panel detach and reset specialist — we handle one thing, and we do it exceptionally well. Our team removes solar panels for roof replacement projects across the greater Phoenix metro, Scottsdale, Chandler, Gilbert, Mesa, Tempe, Glendale, Peoria, and beyond.

Arizona's intense heat and UV exposure mean roofs in the Phoenix valley typically need replacement every 15–20 years — and with solar adoption rates among the highest in the nation, solar panel removal for reroofing has become one of the most common service requests in the state. Unlike general solar companies that treat detach and reset as an afterthought, we've built our entire business around doing it safely, quickly, and affordably.

Our licensed crew (ROC #331356) carefully disconnects your system, labels and stores every component on-site, and reinstalls everything to manufacturer specifications once your new roof is complete. We verify the system is producing power before we leave. The result: a seamless roof replacement experience with zero solar headaches — and 455+ five-star Google reviews to back it up.
