Company Overview

About Solar Detach & Reset

Solar Detach & Reset (operating as Solar Detach Pro LLC) is Arizona's dedicated solar panel detach and reset specialist. Founded to serve the gap between the solar and roofing industries, we provide professional solar panel removal and reinstallation for homeowners undergoing roof replacement across Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tucson, Flagstaff, and all of Maricopa County. We hold Arizona ROC License #331356, carry full liability insurance, and back all work with a 5-year workmanship warranty.

As one of the only companies in Arizona exclusively focused on solar detach and reset — rather than full solar installation — we've built deep expertise in safe panel handling, waterproof mount sealing, and system verification. Our 4.8-star rating across 455+ Google reviews reflects the trust homeowners and roofing contractors place in our team.

What We Do

Our Services

Flagship Service

Solar Panel Detach & Reset

Complete removal and reinstallation of solar panels for homeowners replacing their roofs. We safely disconnect, store, and remount your system — ensuring full waterproofing and electrical integrity throughout.

Solar Panel Inspection

A comprehensive 5-point inspection report assessing panel condition, mounting integrity, wiring, shading factors, and overall system health — giving homeowners and buyers a clear picture of their solar asset.

Solar Bird Guard Installation

Professional installation of critter-proof mesh barriers beneath solar arrays to prevent birds, pigeons, and rodents from nesting — protecting panel longevity and roof integrity without voiding warranties.
