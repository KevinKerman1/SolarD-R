About Our Scottsdale Service

Solar Panel Detach & Reset in Scottsdale, AZ

Scottsdale is home to one of the highest concentrations of residential solar in Arizona — and for good reason. With over 300 days of sunshine per year and some of the most favorable net metering policies in the state, Scottsdale homeowners have embraced solar energy at a remarkable rate. From the gated estates of North Scottsdale to mid-century homes near Old Town, solar panels are a fixture on rooftops across the city. But when those roofs need replacing — as Arizona's intense UV exposure and monsoon seasons demand every 15–20 years — solar panel removal for roof replacement becomes the first step in the process.

What makes Scottsdale unique compared to other Arizona markets is the prevalence of tile roofs in HOA communities. Spanish clay tile, concrete tile, and Mediterranean-style roofing dominate neighborhoods like DC Ranch, McCormick Ranch, Gainey Ranch, and Troon. These roofs require extra care during solar panel removal — improper technique or the wrong equipment can crack tiles that are no longer manufactured and cost hundreds of dollars to source and replace. Solar Detach & Reset crews are specifically trained in tile-safe removal methods refined over hundreds of Scottsdale jobs. We know how to work around the fragile interlocking edges of Spanish clay tile and the varying thicknesses of concrete tile profiles without leaving your roof worse than we found it.

As a licensed solar contractor (ROC #331356), we serve all of Scottsdale — from the high-end solar installations in North Scottsdale's luxury communities to townhomes near Scottsdale Road and everything in between. We coordinate directly with Scottsdale roofing contractors, so the handoff between solar removal and roof work is seamless. Once your new roof is in, we return, reinstall every panel to manufacturer specifications, seal every mount point properly to prevent water intrusion, and verify the system is producing before we leave. Our 5-year workmanship warranty backs every reset we complete.
