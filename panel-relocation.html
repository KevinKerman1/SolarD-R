Panel Relocation

Solar Panel Relocation Service in Phoenix, Arizona

Sometimes panels need to move — to a different section of the same roof, to a new addition, to a detached garage or patio cover, or to a new outbuilding entirely. We relocate residential solar panels across the Phoenix metro and across Arizona, including the racking, wiring, electrical interconnect, and any required permit work.

Common relocation scenarios we handle: a homeowner who's building a second-story addition that will shade the original array, a new patio cover or RV port that's a better solar surface than the house, panels that need to move off a section of roof that's being modified, or simply moving panels to a different roof orientation for better production.

We assess the new location for structural attachment, run new wiring as needed, and re-tie everything into your existing inverter and AHJ-approved interconnect. We pull the required permits and coordinate inspections.

  • Same-roof reposition or new-structure move
  • Permitting and inspections handled
  • New wiring runs and interconnect tie-in
  • Production verified after relocation
