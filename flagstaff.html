Northern Arizona Roofs Demand a Higher Standard

Flagstaff is unlike any other solar market in Arizona. At 7,000 feet above sea level, roofs here face conditions that simply don't exist in the Valley — heavy snow loads exceeding 40 lbs per square foot, pine needle accumulation that traps moisture, and relentless freeze-thaw cycles that accelerate deterioration of roofing materials and solar mounting hardware alike.

These factors mean Flagstaff homeowners reroof more frequently than their counterparts in Phoenix or Scottsdale. When it's time for a new roof, solar panels must come off first — and that job requires a licensed specialist, not a general contractor unfamiliar with live electrical systems and solar racking.

We serve neighborhoods throughout Flagstaff — from the neighborhoods surrounding Northern Arizona University to Pine Canyon, Continental Country Club, and the older craftsman homes in the historic downtown area. We also travel to nearby communities including Williams, Sedona, and Show Low.
